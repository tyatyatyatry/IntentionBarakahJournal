Barakah Journal

Welcome to your Barakah Journal

A quiet place to begin your day with an intention, end it by looking back, and notice where Barakah shows up in between.

Why this exists

“Actions are only by intentions.” (Bukhari & Muslim)

Barakah isn't about doing more in less time. It is getting better results with the time you have, through sincere intention, connection with Allah, and mindful action. This is not a to-do list. Nothing here needs ticking off. It is here to help you notice, and to nudge you gently.

How to use it

  1. Morning: state an intention. One sentence about what you intend today, and for whom.
  2. See it differently. The journal looks at what you are doing and the clues around it (who it is for, why, what is hard about it), then matches your intention to one of the 21 sources of Barakah. You get one new way to see it and one small thing to carry with you. Tap “Another way to see it” for more. Write in English for the best results; a few Bahasa words are understood too. Where it can, a small free model on your device reads the meaning of what you wrote, so it understands more than exact words. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  3. Night: look back. Choose which intention you are reflecting on, or the day in general, and write freely. Afterwards you get “What's next”: ideas for tomorrow's intention.
  4. Watch your four trees grow. Date palm for Faith & Worship, olive for Character & Heart, pomegranate for People & Society, fig for Life & Sustenance. An intention gives its tree 1 drop; looking back on it gives 2 more.

Up to 7 intentions and 7 reflections a day, so it stays light. Your words stay on this device only; use Backup (top right) to keep a copy.
